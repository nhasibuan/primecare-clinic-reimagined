## Content decisions

The revised site will use Indonesian copy that paraphrases the clinic’s public mother-and-baby-health positioning and emphasizes a respectful care journey. The validated Kotabaru address will replace the placeholder multi-city footer. The KitaLulus listing supports a broad statement that the clinic provides health services, including basic and/or specialist medical services; it does not establish an exhaustive service catalogue.

The site will not publish clinic hours, prices, named clinicians, emergency-care claims, insurance claims, rawat-inap availability, or a public telephone/WhatsApp number. The Instagram search result includes WhatsApp `085215862526`, while the independent listing shows a different phone number, so direct contact details require clinic confirmation before publication.
