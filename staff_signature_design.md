# WhatsApp staff signature template

The clinic uses one **global, administrator-managed** signature template for appointment follow-up drafts. The initial default is:

> Salam hangat,  
> Tim Klinik Berkat Insani  
> Kelumpang Hilir, Kotabaru

The template is a short, editable text field stored independently of patient requests. It is appended to each draft after the scheduling message, remains editable in the final WhatsApp dialog, and must not include free-text appointment notes, medical details, or patient identifiers beyond the existing name, selected service, and preferred date.
